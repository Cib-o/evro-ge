On this page, you will find today's official Euro (EUR) rate in Lari (GEL), according to the National Bank of Georgia. How the EUR/GEL exchange rate is calculated

The National Bank sets the official rate daily, which comes into effect the next day. In banks and exchange offices, the commercial rate often differs slightly from the official one — that's why it's worth comparing conditions before exchanging or transferring money.

The official euro exchange rate is set by the National Bank of Georgia (nbg.gov.ge) every business day. evro.ge uses this data to show you the live rate.
